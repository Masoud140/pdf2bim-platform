## 3. Platform gaps SB42 exposes (before it can run)

1. **Two spans with a pier.** The Python model builds one span between two abutments. Needed: span rows, pier support lines with bearings, pier piles, and a link slab across the pier (deck continuity; girders simply supported). The link slab is the largest new engine item.
2. **Tight compound curve (R 90 / R 180) with chorded, fanned girders.** The alignment engine has arcs; girder ends and support lines need checking against the drawings, because the chord offset at R 90 is about 1.4 m over span 1.
3. **End skews up to about 28° that differ girder by girder** (more than 35° on some SB41 ends; SB42 still to read per girder).
4. **Footpath with pedestrian load (3.6 kPa as used in the report)**: not in the load set yet.
5. **Wind 35 m/s on screens in the critical ULS combination**: wind cases exist (`wind_cases`); the screen area needs reading (1150 / 1151).
6. **Variable slab thickness** from the crest curve: the haunch rows (ADR-054) cover this; values from 0804 / 0805.
7. **Pier pile actions governed by rail impact (1500 / 4000 kN)**: collision loads are not modelled. The pile comparison can only cover axial actions from the superstructure.
